[b]Kingdom Inc. Factory Planner[/b]
Plan production in a separate browser window or on a second monitor while your kingdom runs.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]A dashboard styled to match Kingdom Inc., with live research, unlocks, upgrades and resources.
[*]Production targets for products and objective buildings, with chosen input rates.
[*]Outfitter equipment, compatible enhancements and ordered training stages.
[*]Generate a manual build guide with individual machines or compact groups, right-angle connections, utility routing and node modifier details.
[*]Fullscreen flow view, zoom/pan and rainbow highlighting of direct parents and children.
[*]Workshop floors and building registers organized by nested in-game Containers.
[*]Final products, measured items/second, attributed revenue and scoped container profit/deficit.
[*]Treasury balances and observed net changes; Gold, Silver and Copper icons in that order, without decimal coin amounts.
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]
Windows x64 Mono edition of Kingdom Inc.; [url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases/tag/v5.4.23.5]BepInEx 5.4.23.5 or newer in the 5.x series[/url]; [url=https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/]Python 3.9+[/url] available as the python command; a modern browser.
BepInEx and Python are installed separately.

[b]Manual installation[/b]
Close the game. With BepInEx installed, extract the main ZIP into the folder containing KingdomInc.exe, merging BepInEx and _Mods. Start the game, load your kingdom, then double-click Start Factory Planner.cmd. The dashboard opens at http://127.0.0.1:17841. Use Stop Factory Planner.cmd to stop its background web server.
The included README covers setup, updates, removal and troubleshooting. Vortex deployment is untested.

[b]What is measured and what is planned?[/b]
API 0.1.3 records actual accepted transfers, objective deliveries and building earnings/spend. Container rates use live samples over up to 60 seconds; imported or shared production costs are not allocated. Unattributable revenue stays unavailable. Treasury net includes purchases, refunds and collections.
Generated layouts are estimates and manual build guides: they do not place buildings or change your kingdom. Machine counts assume continuous operation and do not credit productivity or byproducts.

[b]Local operation[/b]
The API and dashboard bind to localhost. The API is read-only; plans and cached snapshots are saved in your browser. No save or personal browser data is included in the download.
